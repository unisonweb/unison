# 2026-10-11: FFI pointer reads and writes

The `FFI.Ptr` reads and writes (`get`, `getAt`, `set`, `setAt` of every element type) are
generated inline ("Pointers" under "Arrays and refs" in internals.md). Optimized build,
standalone (`scratchpad/aff/ptr.md`, the suite's new row on its own), medians of two.

| | interpreter | JIT, pointers as call-outs (`UNISON_JIT_DISABLE=ptr`) | JIT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FFI.Ptr: 4 writes and 6 reads, 100000 times | 28.9 ms | 29.0 ms | 0.539 ms |

Ten accesses per iteration: 29 ns each as a call-out (the round trip, the convention's
decoding and the `peek`), about half a nanosecond inline, where the loads and stores sit in
the loop like the byte-array row's (`MutableByteArray: 4 writes and 6 reads`, 488 µs for the
same shape). Other rows are unaffected: the change touches only these foreign calls.

Found by the tests: the interpreter's `Int8`, `Int16`, `Int32` and `Int64` foreign conventions
wrote their results back with the `Nat` type tag, so `FFI.Ptr.Int8.get` of a stored -5 printed
as 18446744073709551611; fixed in the same commit (d728ba4e9).
