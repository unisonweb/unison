# 2026-10-15: records

Trunk's structural records merged and made native ("Records" in internals.md). Optimized
build, a builtins-only transcript (`scratchpad/recbench2/rec.md`: the suite's new row with its
own timer, since `jit_codebase` can't be opened by this build, see below), seven samples of
ten calls each; the table shows the median of the last five.

| | interpreter | JIT, records as exits (`UNISON_JIT_DISABLE=records`) | JIT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Records: a literal, an update, a pattern and two getters, 100000 times | 91.1 ms | 93.0 ms | 7.9 ms |

Per iteration: a three-field literal, a getter, an update, a two-field pattern over the
three-field record, a two-field and a one-field literal with a getter on each, and the
arithmetic; 910 ns in the interpreter, 79 ns native, with every field read paying the walk of
the value's positions map through `unison_jit_rec_pos` (two or three nodes here).
"A field's slot without the map walk" in optimization-ideas.md is the next step if records
turn up on a hot path. Records as exits cost the same as the interpreter, so the instructions
were the whole loop: the first two native samples (15.6 and 18.3 ms) are the compile.

The records stanza of jit-tests.md takes 15 exits in eager mode, none from a record
instruction: ten are the comparisons of two `Any` records of different shapes (left to the
interpreter by design), the rest `Value.value` and the equality of two `Value`s.

The benchmark suite's `jit_codebase` is at schema 26; trunk bumped the codebase schema to 27
(cbc884651) with no migration, so this build refuses it. The suites need a rebuilt codebase
before the next full run.
