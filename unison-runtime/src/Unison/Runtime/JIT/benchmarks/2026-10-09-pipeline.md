# A lighter pass pipeline (2026-10-09)

`default<O2>` was half of a module's compile time ([O0](2026-10-09-o0.md)). This is the
search for a pipeline that produces the same code for less, on the suite, optimized build,
`--jit on`, two to four runs per pipeline. All through `UNISON_JIT_PASSES`.

## The candidates

- **A**: `function(mem2reg,sroa,early-cse,simplifycfg,instcombine),cgscc(inline),function(sroa,early-cse,gvn,instcombine,dse,simplifycfg)`.
- **B**: A with `loop-mssa(licm)` (not in the table; like A).
- **C**: A with the second function group nested inside `cgscc(inline, ...)`, as `O2` does it,
  so that a callee is simplified before its callers consider inlining it, plus `licm`.
- **D**: C plus `ipsccp` before and `function-attrs` inside the call-graph pass.
- **F**: D plus `jump-threading`, `correlated-propagation`, `loop-rotate`, `sccp`, `adce`, and
  `early-cse<memssa>`.
- **H**: F plus `simple-loop-unswitch`, `loop-unroll` (with an `instcombine` after it) and
  `globaldce` at the end. This is `defaultPasses` in `Config.hs` now.
- **O1**: `default<O1>`.

## What each step bought, found by comparing the optimized IR

- A left the callee of the cross-definition row as a separate function: the inliner saw it
  before it was simplified. C nests the simplification inside the inliner's pass (the shape of
  `O2`'s pipeline) and inlines it, but the row stayed slow.
- The row was slow under C because the switch on the inlined callee's status (constant on
  every incoming edge after inlining) and the loop's shape were left as generated;
  `jump-threading`, `correlated-propagation` and `loop-rotate` fold the one and rotate the
  other, and `ipsccp` plus `function-attrs` give the callees' attributes (`nofree`,
  `captures(none)`, return ranges) that `O2`'s output had. D fixed the row, F kept it fixed.
- The closure-call row (`applyN`) and the array row stayed 20 to 27% slower through D and F
  with no difference in the hot functions' IR; what `O2` did and they didn't was peel the
  first iteration of the loop that calls the closure (`loop-unroll`), which takes the
  closure's checks out of the loop. H has it, and matches `O2` on every row.
- The fast entries (`_wf`) of workers that had been inlined everywhere survived as dead
  functions without `globaldce`, costing code generation for nothing.

The text and bytes rows are 6 to 13% faster under every light pipeline, consistently; not
investigated (less peeling and unrolling, so smaller code, is the guess).

Correctness: the test transcript is idempotent under H in the default, threshold-1 and eager
modes, with the stress combination, and with workers disabled.

Takeaways. The pipeline that matches `O2` on the suite does 60% of its pass work, which
is a fifth of the suite's compile time. Each pass that was left out and then needed was found
by diffing the optimized IR of the row that regressed, which is the method to keep: a
regression on a new program means a pass `O2` has and this doesn't, and `UNISON_JIT_PASSES=default<O2>`
tells in one run whether that is the cause.
